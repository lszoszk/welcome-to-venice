Welcome to Venice

La Serenissima, the city built on water

About

Venice spreads across 118 small islands in a lagoon in northern Italy, linked by more than 400 bridges and divided by about 150 canals. There are no cars. You get around on foot or by boat.

Places to see

  • St Mark's Square, with the Basilica and the Campanile
  • The Grand Canal, best seen from a vaporetto
  • Rialto Bridge and the nearby market
  • Murano and Burano, for glass and colourful houses

Good to know

Walk away from the main routes and the city gets quiet fast. Wear comfortable shoes, and expect to get lost at least once.
